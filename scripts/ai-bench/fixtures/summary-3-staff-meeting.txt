Minutes of the monthly academic coordination meeting, attended by subject heads and the vice principal, for circulation to the staff notice board.

The meeting opened with a review of the mid-term assessment results across all sections of grade IX, which showed a general decline in mathematics scores compared to the previous term, averaging about six percentage points lower across sections. The mathematics department attributed this partly to the compressed syllabus pace needed to cover the geometry unit before the term-end exam, and proposed adding one extra period per week for geometry practice starting next month, which the vice principal approved on a trial basis for the remainder of the term.

The science department raised a concern about laboratory equipment shortages, specifically a lack of sufficient microscopes for the grade VII biology practicals, with only twelve working units available for a class size of forty students, forcing groups of three to four students per microscope instead of the recommended pair. A purchase requisition for eight additional microscopes was discussed and will be forwarded to the administration office for budget approval before the next academic year.

The English department shared positive feedback on the new reading circle initiative introduced this term, where students across grades VI to VIII have been assigned peer reading groups that meet twice a week during the library period; initial feedback from students has been enthusiastic, and the department plans to expand the initiative to grade V next term based on this term's results.

A discussion followed regarding the increase in late homework submissions noted by multiple subject teachers, particularly after long weekends, and it was agreed that a consistent late-submission policy should be communicated to parents through the next newsletter rather than each teacher handling it differently. The vice principal will draft the wording for the newsletter in consultation with the discipline committee.

The meeting closed with a reminder about the upcoming annual day rehearsal schedule, which will require some afternoon periods to be adjusted over the next three weeks, and subject heads were asked to inform their respective teachers so that lesson plans can be adjusted accordingly without falling behind on syllabus coverage. The next coordination meeting is scheduled for the same time next month.
