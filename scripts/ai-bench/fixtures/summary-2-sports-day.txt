Sports Day coordinator's notes, compiled for the principal's end-of-year report on the annual athletics meet.

Participation this year was the highest the school has recorded in the last five years, with nearly ninety percent of students across grades IV to XII taking part in at least one track or field event. The 100-metre and 200-metre sprint events saw particularly strong turnout, requiring an extra heat to be added to each age category to accommodate all entries, which slightly delayed the morning schedule by about twenty minutes.

The long jump and shot put pits needed last-minute maintenance the evening before the event, as heavy rain the previous week had left the sand compacted and uneven; the groundskeeping staff worked late to restore proper conditions, and no events were affected on the day itself. The relay races were the highlight of the afternoon session, with the senior boys' 4x100m relay finishing in a time that broke the inter-house record set three years ago, prompting loud cheering from all four houses.

House Blue won the overall championship trophy this year, narrowly edging out House Green by twelve points, largely on the strength of their performance in field events and the junior girls' category, where they swept gold in three out of four events. House Red showed marked improvement over last year's standings, moving up from fourth place to third, attributed by the sports staff to a more consistent training schedule introduced this term.

Medical support was required twice during the day: one student experienced mild dehydration during the afternoon heat and was given water and rest in the shade under the nurse's supervision, recovering within half an hour, and one minor ankle sprain occurred during the high jump event, with the student receiving first aid and being advised to see a physiotherapist as a precaution. Both incidents were documented separately in the school's incident log as per protocol.

Parent attendance was strong for the afternoon prize distribution, and several parents volunteered informally to help manage the refreshment stalls, which the PTA has suggested formalizing into a proper roster for next year. The sports department recommends starting event registration two weeks earlier next year to avoid the last-minute surge that caused the extra sprint heats, and suggests reviewing the shot put and long jump pit drainage before the next monsoon season to prevent similar maintenance issues.
